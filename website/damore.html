D'amore Chocolates

Indulgence, Perfected

Released 2025 | Branding/Packaging Design

Description

This is a paper bag design for a fictional chocolate brand, among the likes of Ghirardelli or Lindt. The simplicity speaks to the quality, as the customers don't need flashy or attention grabbing colors or patterns to be drawn in, but the chocolate dripping from the top gives the viewer a bit of eye candy as well. The name of the brand, D'amore, is an amalgamation of D.A.M. (me) and amore (Italian for love). Because everyone knows when you think of love you think of flowers. Wait, I meant chocolate.

I like chocolate considerably less because of this project.
