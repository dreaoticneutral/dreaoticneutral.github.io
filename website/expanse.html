EXPANSE: The Essential EXO Collection

A box design for a theoretical 'greatest hits' compilation album for EXO, styled in EXO's typical hexagonal fashion.

Released 2025 | Album/Packaging Design

Description

The front of the box has a special logo/cover design that utilizes many elements and concepts from the group's past, the entire design coming together as a gateway of sorts to EXO PLANET, part of the group's unique lore. The back has a more standard EXO logo for those who prefer the classics. The side of the box echoes the group’s motto. The phrase, “We are one, we are EXO,” originally part of their grand concept, evolved into an expression of unity and support among both the members and the fanbase.

The printed box would've looked a lot better if I could afford better paper. Maybe I'll reprint it one day.
