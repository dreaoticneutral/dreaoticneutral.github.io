AIGA 2024 Conference Poster

An unofficial poster created to advertise the 2024 AIGA design conference

Released 2024 | Typography/Poster Design

The margins: Opportunities that arise where design can thrive. The visual design is based on music promotion, styled around record sleeves. The cover on the front gives the basic information on the conference, and the tracks on the back give the more detailed information (scheduling, speakers, sponsors, etc).
